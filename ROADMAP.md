# ReplayTutor roadmap

## v0.1 Alpha

- Reliable local install and bundled BTCUSDT demo.
- BTCUSDT/ETHUSDT spot and USDT perpetual automatic data coverage.
- Deterministic replay, paper execution, ledger, and evidence review.
- English and Simplified Chinese public experience.
- Reproducible Remotion product demo.

## Next

- Expand translation coverage and contributor tooling.
- Additional market-rule adapters with shared conformance tests.
- Better dataset coverage and provenance management.
- Usability and performance improvements from real local trials.

## Explicitly not planned for Alpha

Live order routing, guaranteed-return analytics, cloud credential custody, or autonomous Agent trading.
